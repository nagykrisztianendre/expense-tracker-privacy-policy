About These Pages

These public pages are provided for Google Play compliance and user transparency. They explain how Household Expense Tracker handles user data, the terms for using the app, and how users can delete their account.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel any subscription purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Subscriptions must be canceled separately through the platform where they were purchased. See Account Deletion for details.

The app is subscription-first and there is no free trial. Premium users are ad-free. Eligible non-premium users may see banner ads on Home and Expenses only.
